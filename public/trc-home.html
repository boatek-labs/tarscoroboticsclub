Questions

FAQ

Do I need to buy any equipment to start?
No equipment needed to start. All lessons work in your browser. When you join the club and start building, the club provides components for approved projects.
Is TRC only for students who already know electronics?
Absolutely not. The Beginner path starts from zero — no prior knowledge of electronics, coding, or robotics is assumed. If you can follow instructions, you can build robots.
What's the difference between a Student and a Club Member?
All TARSCO students get a free account with full access to lessons, quizzes, the community, and resources. Club Members are verified by admin and additionally get project team access, the ability to submit projects for the club portfolio, and internal club updates.
How do I apply for club membership?
After creating a student account, go to your dashboard and click "Apply for Club Membership". Admins review applications and respond within a few days.
Can I submit my own robotics project?
Yes — verified club members can submit projects through the dashboard. Each submission goes through an admin approval step before being published to the public projects page.

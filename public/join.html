How It Works

1
Create a free student account

Sign up with your TARSCO school email. Takes about 30 seconds. Instant access to all lessons, the community, and resources.

2
Start learning

Begin with Introduction to Robotics — 8 lessons, no prior experience required. Follow the structured path at your own pace.

3
Apply for club membership (optional)

From your student dashboard, click "Apply for Club Membership". Write a short motivation statement — why do you want to be a TRC member? What do you want to build?

4
Wait for executive review

A club executive reviews your application within 2–5 days. You'll receive a notification and message when a decision is made. Approved members get immediate access to all member privileges.

About TRC

TARSCO Robotics Club was founded by students who believed that real engineering shouldn't wait until university. We compete, we build, and we teach — right here at TARSCO School.

Our Mission

TRC exists to make hands-on robotics engineering accessible to every student at TARSCO School — regardless of background or prior experience. We provide structured learning, real hardware, mentorship, and a community where building something that breaks and fixing it is celebrated, not punished.

What We Do

🎓
Teach

Structured beginner-to-advanced modules, workshops, and lab sessions covering electronics, Arduino, sensors, AI, and competition robotics.

🏗️
Build

Members design and build real robots — from simple obstacle-avoidance cars to AI-powered trash sorters and 3-DOF robotic arms.

🏆
Compete

We enter regional robotics competitions every year. TRC has placed in the top 3 in Line Follower, Sumo Robot, and Obstacle Course categories.

Quick Answers

Do I need school equipment to join?
No equipment needed to create an account or start learning. For physical builds, the club provides components to verified members during lab sessions.
Is TRC free?
Yes. The platform, lessons, community, and resources are completely free for all TARSCO students. Club membership is by application — there are no fees.
I'm not good at electronics. Can I still join?
Absolutely. The Beginner path starts from absolute zero. No prior electronics, coding, or maths knowledge is assumed. If you can follow instructions and ask questions, you can build robots.
